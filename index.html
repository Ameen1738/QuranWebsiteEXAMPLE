About Us

At Al Huda Quran Academy, our mission is to make Quran learning easy, accessible, and structured for students of all ages around the world. We provide online Quran education with qualified male and female teachers who are experienced in teaching Tajweed, recitation, and memorisation in a patient and supportive environment. Our goal is to help every student build a strong connection with the Holy Quran and improve their recitation step by step with proper guidance.

Courses Offered

We offer a range of Quran learning programs suitable for beginners, intermediate, and advanced students. For example:

  • Noorani Qaida (Beginner reading foundation)
  • Quran Reading with Tajweed
  • Hifz-ul-Quran (Memorisation program)
  • Islamic Studies for Children
  • Advanced Recitation Practice
